Our first trial of *live* influenza forecasting for metropolitan Melbourne is now available [online](https://mathmodelling.sph.unimelb.edu.au/~rgm/flu-forecasts/), using publicly available [surveillance data](https://ideas.health.vic.gov.au/).
It will be very exciting to see how the forecasts evolve in the coming months!

And yes, the animated plots are built with [`D3.js`](https://d3js.org/).
