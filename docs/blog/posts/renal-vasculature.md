Bearing the plain title of [Renal Circulation](https://doi.org/10.1002/cphy.cp020306) (2011), this review article provides great detail about renal microcirculation and autoregulation.
It's probably just as useful for the references as for the actual content.

**Update:** I didn't realise that this is a chapter from *Handbook of Physiology* that is now available as part of [Comprehensive Physiology](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/book/10.1002/cphy, and that this body of work is being [regularly updated](https://www.comprehensivephysiology.com/).
