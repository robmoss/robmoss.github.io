Several papers that discuss the complexities of kidney oxygenation and the utility of mathematical models.

- [Intrarenal oxygenation: unique challenges and the biophysical basis of homeostasis](https://doi.org/10.1152/ajprenal.90230.2008) (2008);

- [Methods for studying the physiology of kidney oxygenation](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1440-1681.2008.05063.x) (2008); and

- [A mathematical model of diffusional shunting of oxygen from arteries to veins in the kidney](https://doi.org/10.1152/ajprenal.00544.2010) (2011).
